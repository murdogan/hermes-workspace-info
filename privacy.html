Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Gmail and Google Calendar integration called Hermes Mail Automation, operated by Murat for personal use.

Data accessed and purposes

Depending on granted permissions and the task, the assistants may access message bodies, headers, recipients, labels, attachments and drafts, and calendar event titles, descriptions, dates, locations and attendees. Data is used to perform requested mail and calendar tasks, prepare summaries and execute authorized scheduled work. Only permissions needed for enabled features should be requested.

Processing and sharing

Selected content needed for a task may be sent to the configured AI inference provider (currently OpenAI through the Codex integration). Requested results may be delivered through Telegram, and authorized messages or calendar invitations may be delivered to their recipients. These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies. The operator does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Google API data is not used by this application to develop or train generalized AI models.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials are stored on the operator’s server with restricted file permissions, separate from this public website. Assistant profiles may retain protected credential copies. Conversation history, task logs, downloaded attachments, generated reports and backups may contain task-related data. Retention varies by artifact; this application does not claim automatic deletion after a fixed period. Access is limited through the server and bot access controls.

Control, revocation and deletion

The account holder can revoke access at Google Account connections. Revocation prevents further authorized API access but does not automatically delete previously stored outputs or backups. The operator can remove local credentials, conversation records, attachments and outputs and manage backup retention. Provider-side copies are subject to the relevant provider’s retention policies.

Google API commitments

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Human access is limited to the account holder/operator and circumstances permitted by that policy.

Contact and updates

Contact the operator through this project’s issue tracker. Do not post tokens, email contents, attachments or other private information publicly. Material changes to this integration will be reflected on this page.
